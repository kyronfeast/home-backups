home-backups privacy policy

Last updated 29 September 2026.

home-backups is used only by its owner, Thomas Short, for his own household's backups.

What it accesses

It uses Google's drive.file permission. It can read, create, change and delete only the files it created in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot see any other Drive files, email, photos or contacts.

What it stores

Encrypted backup files in the owner's own Google Drive. Nothing is stored anywhere else and nothing is shared with anyone.

What it collects

Nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no data is sold or given to third parties.

Removing access

The owner can remove its access at any time in Google Account settings, under Security, Third-party apps and services.
